# Working in this tree

Read `AGENTS.md` as well; everything in it applies.

- Do not stop to say what you are going to do next.  Do it.
- Do not end a reply with what is coming, what is left, what you would
  do next, or an offer to carry on.  A reply says what was built and
  what was verified, and stops there.  The plan lives in
  `doc/osprey-progress.ms`; that is where what is left is written down,
  and it is the only place it belongs.
- When a piece of work is finished, start the next one from that plan in
  the same turn.  Keep going until everything the two Osprey sources
  describe is implemented.
- Do not ask for input, confirmation, or a choice between approaches.
  Decide, build it, test it against a booted kernel, and say plainly in
  the man page and in the commit what is real and what is only
  demonstrated.
- Keep `doc/osprey-progress.ms` current as each piece lands.
